What Is Call Option and Put Option?

Simple explanation for beginners in India

Call Option and Put Option are the most basic concepts of options trading. Many beginners lose money because they trade without understanding these concepts clearly. This article explains Call and Put options in a simple and educational way.

What Is a Call Option?

A Call Option gives the buyer the right, but not the obligation, to buy a stock or index at a fixed price before a specific expiry date.

Call Options are generally associated with rising markets. When traders expect the market to move up, they try to understand how Call Options work.

What Is a Put Option?

A Put Option gives the buyer the right, but not the obligation, to sell a stock or index at a fixed price before expiry.

Put Options are generally associated with falling markets. Traders look at Put Options when they expect the market to go down.

Difference Between Call Option and Put Option

Feature Call Option Put Option
Market View Upward (Bullish) Downward (Bearish)
Right Buy at fixed price Sell at fixed price
Used When Market expected to rise Market expected to fall

Simple Example for Beginners

Suppose an index is trading at a certain level. A Call Option generally becomes valuable if the market moves upward, while a Put Option generally becomes valuable if the market moves downward.

Option prices depend on many factors such as market movement, time to expiry, and volatility.

Risks in Call and Put Options

Options trading involves high risk. Prices can change rapidly, and beginners may lose their entire premium if they trade without proper knowledge.
