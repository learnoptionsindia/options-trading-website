Is Options Trading Safe for Beginners?

Important risks every beginner must understand

Many beginners ask whether options trading is safe in India. The answer depends on knowledge, risk management, and experience.

Why Options Trading Is Risky

  • High leverage
  • Time decay
  • Market volatility

Common Beginner Mistakes

Beginners often trade without understanding concepts like expiry, lot size, and premium decay.

How Beginners Should Start

Beginners should focus on learning concepts, using paper trading, and understanding risks before trading with real money.
