What Is NIFTY Options Trading?

Beginner guide for Indian stock market learners

NIFTY Options Trading is one of the most popular segments in the Indian stock market. Many beginners are attracted to NIFTY options because of high liquidity and weekly expiry.

What Is NIFTY?

NIFTY 50 is an index that represents the top 50 companies listed on the National Stock Exchange of India.

What Are NIFTY Options?

NIFTY options are derivative contracts based on the NIFTY index. These include Call Options and Put Options.

Why Beginners Choose NIFTY Options

  • High liquidity
  • Weekly expiry
  • Popular among traders

Risks in NIFTY Options Trading

Options trading involves high risk. Prices can change rapidly, and beginners may lose money if they trade without proper knowledge.
